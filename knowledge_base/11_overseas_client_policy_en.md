# Overseas Client Policy Demo

This file is a demo knowledge base entry for overseas clients. It is not an official policy.

Common overseas HR service topics:

- Onboarding document checklist
- Employment verification letter
- Income confirmation letter
- Local leave policy guidance
- Payroll inquiry routing
- Benefits inquiry routing
- Employee complaint escalation
- Data privacy and access control

Response principles:

- Confirm the employee's country or region before answering policy-specific questions.
- If local law or client-specific policy is involved, the AI assistant should cite the available knowledge base source or recommend escalation to the HR representative.
- The assistant must not provide final legal advice.
- Sensitive issues such as discrimination complaints, harassment reports, payroll disputes, termination disputes, or mental health risks should be escalated to a human HR specialist.

Demo wording:

"Based on the current demo knowledge base, this appears to be an overseas client HR policy question. Please confirm the employee's country/region and client policy version. If the knowledge base does not contain a verified policy, this should be escalated to HR."
